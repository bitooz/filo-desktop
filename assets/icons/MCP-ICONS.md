# Connector logos

The ready-made apps' logos (Extensions › Apps), bundled so nothing is fetched at run time. Each is the brand's own mark,
used to name the service it stands for; the trademarks are their owners'. `connectors_store::preset_icon` draws them.

| File | Mark | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mcp-linear.svg`, `mcp-stripe.svg`, `mcp-huggingface.svg`, `mcp-cloudflare-docs.svg` | In the brand's colour | [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.34.0 (CC0), its paths and official hex colours |
| `mcp-notion.svg`, `mcp-vercel.svg`, `mcp-github.svg`, `mcp-sentry.svg` | One colour, drawn in the theme's text colour (black by brand, so it shows on dark too) | Simple Icons 16.34.0 (CC0) |
| `mcp-context7.svg`, `mcp-context7-dark.svg` | Its square mark, light and dark | Context7's own logo files on context7.com (`context7-logo-light/dark.svg`), the wordmark left out |
| `mcp-playwright.svg` | As published | playwright.dev/img/playwright-logo.svg |
| `../images/mcp-deepwiki.png` | As published (180 px) | deepwiki.com's `apple-icon.png`, via the Internet Archive's copy of 1 Jan 2026 (the site refused direct downloads) |
| `mcp-upsy.svg`, `mcp-upsy-dark.svg` | Its tile, light and dark | upsy.ai's `icon.svg`, its two colour schemes as two files (no CSS) |
| `../images/mcp-executor.png`, `../images/mcp-executor-dark.png` | As published (192 px); dark is it inverted, as the site does in dark mode | executor.sh's `favicon-192.png` |
| `mcp-clickup.svg` | In the brand's colour | Exported once from the phone app's connector icons (its `mcp-icons.ts`) |

Files (a folder on this Mac) and custom servers have no brand: a folder, a globe (remote) or a terminal (local).
